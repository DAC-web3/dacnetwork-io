Why DACnetwork?

The Problem

Today's digital economy creates enormous value, yet millions of people contribute every day without receiving fair recognition or ownership of the value they generate. People walk, create content, build communities, operate devices, share knowledge and contribute to digital ecosystems, while the economic benefits are often captured by centralized platforms.

Traditional platforms monetize user activity through advertising, data collection and centralized control. Users generate value but rarely participate in the long-term growth of the ecosystems they help build.

Blockchain technology introduced the possibility of digital ownership, but much of the crypto industry has focused on speculation rather than real-world utility. DACnetwork was created to address this imbalance.

The DACnetwork Ecosystem

An Ecosystem, Not a Single Application

DACnetwork is designed as a decentralized ecosystem where multiple independent products operate under one shared digital economy powered by the $DAC utility token. Each product solves a different real-world problem while contributing to the long-term growth of the ecosystem.

Ecosystem Architecture

rutaX transforms verified physical movement into digital value. The application rewards users for measurable real-world activity using GPS validation and advanced anti-fraud technologies. Rewarded activities include walking, running, cycling and hiking. Privacy remains a core principle — sensitive location information is processed locally whenever possible.

DACmeta — Digital Society

DACmeta represents the digital social layer of DACnetwork. Rather than building a traditional gaming metaverse, DACmeta creates structured virtual environments where people, organizations and communities interact through decentralized ownership. Capabilities include virtual meeting spaces, educational environments, NFT galleries, a digital asset marketplace, and a creator economy.

DAC Smart Systems — DePIN

DAC Smart Systems extends DACnetwork beyond software into the physical world. Edge devices perform local processing while preserving user privacy. Instead of transmitting unnecessary personal information to centralized servers, devices verify operational events and publish only cryptographic proof when required.

DACx — Private Communication Layer

DACx is the private communication and identity layer of DACnetwork. It enables members to communicate through ephemeral messaging and a self-custody Solana Devnet wallet — identified by a unique xID, without a phone number or email. DACx is live on Android (Beta v1.5.7) with OTA updates. Voice calls exist in the current UI. They are not E2EE. $DAC mainnet payments inside DACx are a future milestone.

Building a Circular Digital Economy

The DACnetwork economy follows a simple principle: Contribution → Reward → Utility → Participation → Growth. This cycle creates continuous ecosystem activity. As participation increases, ecosystem utility expands, attracting new participants and creating a self-reinforcing growth loop.

Real activity creates value.

Utility sustains value.

Every major participant benefits when the ecosystem grows. Users benefit from additional services. Creators gain access to larger audiences. Organizations benefit from stronger infrastructure. 16.1 What is DACx?

DACx is the private communication and identity layer of the DACnetwork ecosystem. It is how DACnetwork members speak to each other — directly, by mutual consent, and ephemerally.

DACx solves one of the most fundamental problems in digital communication: the permanent surveillance economy. Traditional messaging platforms require personal identification, store conversations indefinitely and monetize user data. DACx was built on the opposite philosophy.

DACx is currently live on Android (Beta v1.5.7) with OTA updates and is part of the DACnetwork ecosystem at dacnetwork.io. Releases: https://github.com/DAC-web3/dacx/releases

16.3 The xID System

At the core of DACx is the xID — a 9-digit unique network identity that replaces traditional personal identification methods. Users create an xID without providing a phone number, email address or any personal information.

The xID becomes the user's permanent identity within DACnetwork. It is linked to their self-custody Solana wallet, enabling seamless ecosystem participation from a single, privacy-preserving identity.

16.4 Ephemeral Communication Philosophy

DACx is built on a fundamental principle: communication belongs to the moment it was created, not to a permanent archive controlled by a platform.

Ephemeral messaging means that once a message has been read, it disappears after 3 minutes. Unread messages are held on DACx servers until they are read, then deleted. There is no permanent conversation archive. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted yet — that is a future roadmap item.

This is not a limitation. It is a feature. It aligns DACx with the broader DACnetwork philosophy: technology should serve the user, not surveil them.

16.5 Built-in Solana Wallet

DACx includes a self-custody Solana wallet. For new accounts the key is created on the phone and recovered with 12 words (the phrase never leaves the device). The wallet is bound to the user xID and unlocked with a PIN.

Today the wallet runs on Solana Devnet. New accounts create the key on the phone and recover it with 12 words. Devnet DAC (mint 8NeGLu1s8jQjvrtgpTKPMkGg71NKKpwaMthhUSkfLatR) can be viewed and sent. This is not mainnet $DAC. Mainnet $DAC payments inside DACx are a planned Phase 2+ milestone.

Self-custody: only you control your private keys.

PIN-secured: protected by your personal code.

Solana-native: Devnet today (DAC + SOL). Mainnet $DAC is a future milestone.

xID-bound: wallet and identity in one.

16.9 Privacy Commitment

DACx does not collect phone numbers. DACx does not collect email addresses. DACx does not sell user data. DACx does not monetize user behavior. The recovery phrase is never sent to the server. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted yet. Unread messages are held on DACx servers until they are read, then deleted after 3 minutes. After that, conversation content is not kept as a permanent archive.

Every architectural decision in DACx reflects the DACnetwork core principle: technology must serve the user, not exploit them. Communication is a human right. Privacy is a design requirement, not an optional feature.

De ce DACnetwork?

!

Problema

Economia digitala de astazi creeaza o valoare enorma, totusi milioane de oameni contribuie zilnic fara a primi recunoastere echitabila sau proprietate asupra valorii pe care o genereaza. Oamenii merg, creeaza continut, construiesc comunitati, opereaza dispozitive, impart cunostinte si contribuie la ecosisteme digitale, in timp ce beneficiile economice sunt capturate de obicei de platforme centralizate.

Platformele traditionale monetizeaza activitatea utilizatorilor prin publicitate, colectare de date si control centralizat. Utilizatorii genereaza valoare, dar rareori participa la cresterea pe termen lung a ecosistemelor pe care le ajuta sa se construiasca.

Tehnologia blockchain a introdus posibilitatea proprietatii digitale, dar o mare parte din industria crypto s-a concentrat pe speculatie mai degraba decat pe utilitate din lumea reala. DACnetwork a fost creat pentru a aborda acest dezechilibru.

16.4 Filozofia Comunicarii Efemere

DACx este construit pe un principiu fundamental: comunicarea apartine momentului in care a fost creata, nu unei arhive permanente controlate de o platforma.

16.9 Angajamentul pentru Confidentialitate

DACx nu colecteaza numere de telefon. DACx nu colecteaza adrese de email. DACx nu vinde date ale utilizatorilor. DACx nu monetizeaza comportamentul utilizatorilor. Fraza de recuperare nu este trimisa pe server. Mesajele nu sunt inca criptate end-to-end. Mesajele necitite stau pe serverele DACx pana sunt citite, apoi sunt sterse dupa 3 minute. Dupa aceea, continutul conversatiei nu este pastrat ca arhiva permanenta.

Fiecare decizie arhitecturala in DACx reflecta principiul central al DACnetwork: tehnologia trebuie sa serveasca utilizatorul, nu sa il exploateze. Comunicarea este un drept uman. Confidentialitatea este o cerinta de proiectare, nu o functionalitate optionala.
