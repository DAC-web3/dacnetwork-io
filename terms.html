The site presents the DACnetwork ecosystem. Its current status is described honestly and may change:

  • $DAC: a Solana Token-2022 token with a fixed supply of 3,000,000,000 and an automatic 0.5% transfer fee, described as a utility token for access to ecosystem services. It exists on Solana mainnet.

  • DAC Smart Systems: research and development of distributed edge and DePIN infrastructure.

DACx messages are not end-to-end encrypted yet. Unread messages are held on DACx servers until they are read and are then deleted after 3 minutes. Voice calls are not offered in the current interface.
